Comparison of ORA and GSEA Workflows for RNA-seq Enrichment Analysis in R

Introduction

During DSFB1 course, I have learned how to perform ORA Gene Enrichment analysis for an RNA-sequencing experiment and applied it on the provided dataset for the final assessment.

To further deepen my knowledge of RNA analysis, I want to learn how to perform another type of RNA-seq enrichment analysis: GSEA.

In this self-study assignment, I want to:

  • Learn how enrichment methodology affect the biological interpretation of RNA-seq data and compare the previous ORA results with a new GSEA analysis using the same DESeq2 dataset.

  • Obtain a more complete picture of the previously used data.

Background | Difference between GSEA and ORA

Difference between GSEA and ORA: ORA uses a threshold-filtered list of significant genes, while GSEA uses all genes ranked by expression change. ORA is ideal for quick, high-magnitude gene lists, whereas GSEA excels at identifying subtle, coordinated changes in entire pathways.

https://pluto.bio/resources/Learning%20Series/gsea-vs-ora-two-key-pathway-analysis-approaches-for-next-gen-sequencing-data

Research question: Do ORA and GSEA lead to the same biological interpretation of ONECUT1-induced fibroblast transdifferation?

Subquestions: - Are GO terms found by ORA and GSEA the same? - Does neurogenesis/nervous system development still appear? - Do apoptotic processes remain enriched? - Do GSEA find subtler pathways that were missed by ORA? - To what extend do conclusions depend on the padj <0.01 and LFC > 1? - What is the difference in the results of GSEA GO term analysis and KEGG pathways analysis?
